Football Hub

WDD 131 - Website Planning Document

1. Site Name

Football Hub

Football Hub is a website created for football fans who want a simple place to find football information. The name was chosen because the website brings different types of football information together in one central location.

The football symbol will be used as part of the site's visual identity and logo.

2. Site Purpose

The purpose of Football Hub is to provide football fans with useful and easy-to-understand football information in one place. The website will provide information about football teams, competitions, upcoming matches, results, football news, and interesting football facts.

The site will be designed to work well on both mobile devices and larger desktop screens. Visitors will be able to navigate between different sections to find the football information they are interested in.

3. Scenarios

The following questions represent some of the questions that visitors to Football Hub might ask:

  • What football matches are coming up this week?
  • Where can I find information about my favorite football team?
  • What were the latest football results?
  • What major football competitions are currently being played?

4. Color Schema

Football Hub will use a green, white, and gold color scheme. The colors were selected to create a strong sports-focused appearance while keeping the content easy to read.

Dark Green #0B3D2E Header, navigation, main headings
Football Green #176B4D Section accents, borders, buttons
Gold #F4C542 Highlights, important information, accents
White #FFFFFF Content backgrounds and readable areas

The dark green color will be the main brand color. Football green will be used for accents and section borders. Gold will highlight important information, while white will provide clean content areas and contrast.

5. Typography

Montserrat - Headings

Football Hub
Latest Football News and Information

Montserrat will be used for the site title, main headings, section headings, and other important titles. Its strong appearance will help create a clear sports-oriented design.

Roboto - Body Text

Football Hub provides football fans with useful information about teams, matches, competitions, results, and football news.

Roboto will be used for paragraphs, descriptions, navigation text, and other general content because it is clean and easy to read on both mobile and desktop screens.
